## 1. Pitch
A UI-driven pirate-life game set in a One Piece-inspired world, in the spirit of Free Cities
and Fort of Chains: deep systems, lots of choices, emergent stories. You start as a nobody
in a village in one of the four Blues and try to become a feared pirate.
